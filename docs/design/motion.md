## Rules

### MO-01 Content that is already there renders settled

**Rule.** When an element is present on its first render (hydration, reload, a lazily loaded chunk, older history, a remount, a tree/page/scope switch, a reopened pane, Home with cached data), render its final state with no entrance: motion `initial={false}` or no arriving class/attribute. When an item is created while its list is on screen, it may play its entrance, decided once on its first render: `useFresh(createdAt)` is true only within `FRESH_MS` (1000 ms) of creation, and nothing re-decides it on later renders. When a surface has a special entrance (Home arrival, landing hero), it plays on first load only. Which surfaces and lists this covers: TR-02.

**Sources.** [RF] "Often state transitions accidentally play out when the page loads, but this makes the application feel poorly built." · [RF] "When you reload the page, the page shows up instantly without motion." · [LIN] "No longer animates in similar issues if they are available immediately on navigating to an issue." · [OWNER c:1fy] (recorded outcome) (in Chinese, translated: the animation plays on first load only; back on Home, data still in memory shows at once) · [CODE] `frontend/src/motion/useFresh.ts`: "hydrated, lazily loaded, older-history and remounted items render settled"; tests `frontend/src/motion/tokens.test.ts` ("only counts items created within the window") and `frontend/src/components/terminal/PaneMessageList.motion.test.tsx` ("renders hydrated history settled and plays the entrance only for a send made while it is open").

**Michi today.** 24 of 50 `<AnimatePresence>` set `initial={false}`; the other 26 wrap a child that mounts only when opened (menus, dialogs). Messages, agent blocks, receipts, branch rows and anchors decide freshness on first render (census at f578af740). Copy `frontend/src/components/terminal/agentBlockMotion.tsx` (`Reveal`, `RevealList`, `GlyphSwap`: "Whatever is there when its list, disclosure or glyph slot mounts renders settled") and `frontend/src/components/terminal/MessageBlock.tsx` `useBlockEntrance`. Exception: `.t-pre-block` (`frontend/src/index.css`) plays `fadeIn` on every mount, so its comment chips, merge notice and runtime warning replay on hydration.

**Check.** `npm test -w frontend -- src/motion/tokens.test.ts src/components/terminal/PaneMessageList.motion.test.tsx`. Review every new `<AnimatePresence>` around a list or slot for `initial={false}`. Devtools: reload a workspace with history; the Animations panel records nothing except loops and Home's first-load arrival.

### MO-02 Frequent pointer actions stay at the short end

**Rule.** When a pointer action repeats tens of times a day, keep its motion at the floor:
- Hover or colour change on rows, tree items, tabs, chips and icon buttons: `var(--t-quick)` (60 ms) on `var(--t-ease)`, both ways. When a hover reveals a control in lockstep with the row's fill (row kebabs), switch it instantly.
- Hover and keyboard highlight on menu and palette items: `var(--m-hoverMs)` (= `--t-soft`, 100 ms), which `frontend/src/components/ui/Menu.css` sets on every item inside `.michi-menu` or `.michi-menu-scope`. A new menu renders its rows there and writes no transition of its own.
- Hover and state on text buttons and form fields (`.ui-btn`, `.ui-input`, `.ui-select`, the composer frame): `var(--t-soft)` (100 ms) on `var(--t-ease)`.
- Menus and popovers opened by pointer: their primitive's motion (MO-05, TR-12) and nothing longer: a 34 ms hold, 120 ms in (`DUR.brief`), 80 ms out (`DUR.dismiss`); no stagger, no travel. Every desktop menu and anchored popover takes it, the ones on `ActionMenu`, `DbPopover` and `MichiMenu` and the single popovers included, which open in one frame today [OWNER decision 2026-11-14 (TR-Q1)] (TR-13).
- Press: feedback starts at pointer-down with no delay. A press changes colour only, at its control's hover timing, and never moves: no scale and no offset on `:active` [OWNER decision 2026-11-14 (ST-Q2)] (ST-06). The press transforms in the code go, `.t-icon-btn`'s 60 ms scale and the phone composer action's 100 ms one (`frontend/src/components/terminal/mobile.css` `.terminal-composer .t-action-btn`) among them; ST-06 lists all eight. The phone long-press sink before a menu lifts its row belongs to that lift (TR-11), not to press feedback.
- When the pointer is over a list that re-sorts, move rows instantly (`frontend/src/components/terminal/ActivityView.tsx` `RESORT_INSTANT`). When a new update for the same element arrives within 150 ms of the last one, skip its animation and show the new state ([RF]; no Michi code applies this yet).
- No hover or press takes longer than `var(--t-soft)`.

**Sources.** [EK] "Remove animations or hover interactions altogether if they are seen tens, maybe even hundreds of times a day." · [HIG] "In apps, generally avoid adding motion to UI interactions that occur frequently." · [RF] "the native macOS right click menu only animates out, not in, due to the frequent usage of it." · [M3] "In dense products, such as on desktop, menus can open instantly to reduce motion." · [LIN] "Sidebar nav item hover state changes are now instant." · [LIN] (a button) "rather than fading out over 150 milliseconds as it should have." · [RF] "Responding to smaller changes, such as a checkbox toggle or button hover, should be roughly 100ms in duration." · [RF] "Sometimes updates happen with higher frequency than an animation can run." · [RF] (Dev Tools prototype) "const animationDurationMs = 150;" · [CODE] `frontend/src/components/ui/Popover.tsx`: "Menus are opened many times a day: barely-there growth in, a quick fade out." · [CODE] `frontend/src/components/terminal/ActivityView.tsx`: "Under the pointer the list re-sorts at once, so nothing slides under the cursor." · [OWNER] decision 2026-11-14 (TR-Q1): all like the right-click menu, growing and fading in, fading out to leave · [OWNER] decision 2026-11-14 (ST-Q2): only ghost buttons, the borderless ones, press one step darker, mixing 10% toward `--term-fg` · **Conflict:** [EK], [LIN] and [M3] make dense hovers and desktop menus instant, while [LIN] also fades a button's hover-out over 150 ms; Michi fades row hovers over 60 ms, menu and button hovers over 100 ms, and grows menus over 120 ms. **Choice:** keep Michi's values as the ceiling for frequent actions; nothing hovers longer than 100 ms. **Why:** code majority: about 143 hover fades at 60 and 100 ms, none at 150 ms (census at f578af740). The menu grow is not a majority but the shared primitive's documented and tested default (`frontend/src/components/ui/Popover.tsx`, `frontend/src/components/ui/Popover.test.tsx`), and the menus that open in one frame converge on it by the owner's decision (TR-Q1, TR-13). No hard requirement is involved, and [RF]'s 100 ms button hover supports the button, field and menu tier.

**Michi today.** Hover fades: about 100 at `--t-quick` and about 43 at 100 ms (`--t-soft`, or `--m-hoverMs` in menus), mostly `.ui-btn`, inputs and menu items (census at f578af740). Every menu renders its items inside `.michi-menu` or `.michi-menu-scope`: `PopoverSurface` adds `michi-menu` for any `menuKind`, `ContextMenu` defaults to `'context'`, and the command palette's results carry `michi-menu-scope`; so `frontend/src/components/ui/Menu.css` overrides the 60 ms `.ui-menu-item` base everywhere. Row kebabs drop the fade on purpose ("the fader's solid --term-alt strip must appear in lockstep with the row's own bg flip"). Exceptions: menu items fade on plain `ease` while every other state fade uses `--t-ease` (the remaining deviation, MO-07); the Trash menu's items don't fade (`frontend/src/components/terminal/pages/Trash.css` `.trash-menu .ui-menu-item`); the prose chip hover runs 180 ms with box-shadow (`frontend/src/components/terminal/assistant/assistant.css`); the owner's `.mention-chip` hover (`frontend/src/index.css`) runs 180 ms with its glow and stays with the owner's chip look (tokens.md TK-07), but its timing is not copied.

### MO-03 Keyboard navigation and typing don't animate

**Rule.**
- When the keyboard moves a highlight or focus (arrows or j/k in menus, the palette, lists and trees; Tab; switching pane focus), the highlight jumps: no sliding indicator and no transform. Its background or colour may fade over at most `var(--t-soft)` (menus and the palette: `--m-hoverMs`; rows and trees: `--t-quick`).
- When a shortcut opens or closes a surface, it plays the same motion as its pointer-opened form, never a longer variant (TR-05). ⌘K follows MO-20 and ⌘J follows MO-21. A menu opened from the keyboard grows from its anchored corner, since there is no press point.
- When content changes per keystroke (composer height while typing, palette and @ / slash results filtering, the selection bar following edits), show the new state at once: no tween, stagger or follow-up glide.
- One sanctioned exception: the @-menu row settle (`frontend/src/components/MentionEditor.tsx` `ROW_SETTLE`, a 160 ms transform FLIP when a row above stops matching). Tests pin it. Don't copy it.

**Sources.** [EK] "These actions may be repeated hundreds of times a day, an animation would make them feel slow, delayed, and disconnected from the user’s actions." · [EK] "Notice how the highlight feels delayed compared to the keys you press." · [EK] (Raycast) "If it animated every time I opened it, it would be very annoying." · [RF] "I removed motion from core interactions and suddenly felt like I was moving much faster" · [OWNER c:9m] (recorded decision) (in Chinese, translated: ⌘K: the scrim fades in over 180 ms and the panel goes from opacity 0 and scale 0.96 to 1 over 180 ms on (0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1) … it closes with no animation) · [OWNER c:9o] (recorded outcome) (in Chinese, translated: pressing ⌘J mid-animation reverses from where the panel is) · [CODE] `frontend/src/components/ui/Popover.test.tsx` ("grows from its anchored corner when the keyboard opened it after a click elsewhere"); `frontend/src/components/terminal/ComposerShell.test.tsx` ("never animates the card while typing, only after a send"); `frontend/src/components/MentionEditor.menu.test.tsx` ("lets the remaining rows ride up when a row above stops matching"). · **Conflict:** [EK] gives a launcher opened many times a day no motion, and [RF] removed motion from keyboard-driven lists; the owner chose an animated ⌘K entrance and ⌘J panel. **Choice:** the owner's motion for those surfaces; [EK] and [RF] for navigation inside any surface. **Why:** owner decisions are binding; no decision covers navigation inside a surface, and the code already jumps there.

**Michi today.** No sliding highlight exists. `frontend/src/components/terminal/CommandPalette.tsx` rows are menu rows (`.michi-menu-scope`) and change background only, over `--m-hoverMs`. Popover picks the growth origin from the last pointer press and falls back to the anchored side (`growthOrigin`). `frontend/src/components/SelectionActions.tsx` measures its layout morph only when content changes ("typing never animates it").

**Check.** Tests above. `rg -n "translate" frontend/src/components/terminal/CommandPalette.tsx` returns nothing. Devtools: hold ↓ in a long menu; the Animations panel records background transitions only, no transform.

### MO-04 Durations come from motion tokens

**Rule.**
- When you write a duration, use the token for the element type (MO-05): `var(--t-quick|soft|brief|enter|exit|dismiss|move|collapse|scrim-exit|decay|slow)` and `var(--t-loop-fast|loop|loop-slow)` in CSS, and `DUR.*`, `TRANSITION.*`, `SPRING.*`, `KEYBOARD_MOVE` or `LINE_MOTION.*` from `frontend/src/motion/tokens.ts` in JS. Token values and roles: TK-29, TK-30.
- Three more durations get a token in both CSS and JS, and two JS-only tokens get CSS twins [OWNER decision 2026-11-14 (MO-Q1)]:
  - 120 ms: `--t-brief` / `DUR.brief`, for menus and popovers growing in, the selection popup's entrance, status words, labels and digits leaving, status cross-fades and short content fades;
  - 80 ms: `--t-dismiss` / `DUR.dismiss`, for menus, popovers and the selection popup fading out, and inline content fading as it leaves;
  - 220 ms: `--t-move` / `DUR.move`, for on-screen layout moves and settles (re-sort, FLIP followers, held-room release, the change-list settle, the thought finish, the ask card's collapse), pane tracks opening and the canvas camera;
  - 160 ms and 140 ms keep their JS names and gain CSS twins: `--t-collapse` (= `DUR.collapse`) and `--t-scrim-exit` (= `DUR.scrimExit`).
- Write none of these values as a literal, and replace the literals already there. A module constant that times motion with one of them (`MENU_IN_MS`, `PAGE_IN_MS`, `PANE_ENTER_MS`, `RELEASE_MS`, `TWEEN_MS`, `FILL_S`, `COLLAPSE_S`, `GHOST_OUT_S`) takes its value from the token (`DUR.brief * 1000`). Another motion value equal to one takes the same token (the 220 ms path draws and the rail draw-in: `DUR.move`). A timer that only waits (a buffer, a debounce, a hold, or `EXIT_TIMEOUT_MS`, which outlasts DrawerShell's exit) is a dwell and keeps its own named constant (TK-32). DrawerShell's 160 ms exit, the one MO-05 duration CSS had no token for, is `var(--t-collapse)` (MO-06).
- Owner-set values (⌘J 300 ms and 28/14 px [OWNER c:9o]; `KEYBOARD_MOVE` 350 ms [OWNER c:kx, c:lf]) live as named constants next to their one user, or in `frontend/src/motion/tokens.ts` when shared (TK-31). The send glide is a named constant too ([CODE] `frontend/src/components/terminal/sendScroll.ts` `SEND_GLIDE`, 480 ms).
- Never introduce a new literal duration or delay. Dwells and staggers are named constants in JS; a CSS stagger is a `calc()` on an index custom property, as Home's `calc(90ms + var(--home-card-index, 0) * 35ms)` is. A new staggered reveal copies the nearest existing recipe (MO-05, Staggers; TR-29).

**Sources.** [OWNER] decision 2026-11-14 (MO-Q1): add tokens in both CSS and JS, and give 160 ms and 140 ms a CSS version too · [OWNER] decision 2026-11-14 (MO-Q6): copy the nearest existing recipe (cards follow Home, document blocks follow Pages Home) · [CODE] `frontend/src/index.css` token comment: "Every transition and one-shot animation uses one of the first six; every looping animation one of the three loop rhythms." · [CODE] `frontend/src/motion/tokens.ts` `DUR`: "motion only (no CSS counterpart)." (`expand`, `collapse`, `scrimExit`) · [CODE] `frontend/src/motion/tokens.css.test.js` ("mirrors the CSS motion tokens in motion/tokens.ts") · [CODE] the `frontend/eslint.config.mjs` rule warns on inline transition and animation strings with ms/s literals ("Use a motion token").

### MO-09 Motion is interruptible, and leaving elements are inert at once

**Rule.**

### MO-14 Reduced motion: one state, two triggers

**Rule.** Reduced motion is on when the OS reports `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` or Settings › Reduce motion is on (`html[data-reduce-motion='on']`). Each animation's reduced form (MO-15) is identical under either trigger, with no exception: CSS colour and opacity fades land at once under the OS setting too [OWNER decision 2026-11-14 (MO-Q11)].
- CSS: one catch-all serves both triggers. The "App-level reduce motion" block in `frontend/src/index.css` (on every element and pseudo-element: animation and transition durations 0.01 ms, delays 0, one iteration, `scroll-behavior: auto`, all `!important`) applies under `:root[data-reduce-motion='on']` and, with the same declarations, to `*, *::before, *::after` inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`. Its `!important` reaches inline `transition` and `animation` strings too. A transition, keyframe animation or loop whose reduced form is the same change at once, or its own base frame, needs no reduce rule of its own.
- The catch-all only shortens timings; it never changes an end state. - Inline style strings: the catch-all shortens their timing under both triggers. - motion/react components render inside `MotionRoot` (MotionConfig `'always'` for the toggle, `'user'` for the OS), so they reduce the same way under either trigger: x, y, scale, rotate, width, height, top, left and layout animations jump, while opacity, colour, margin, padding, border width, clip-path, filter and pathLength keep animating. - Imperative `animate()`, WAAPI, rAF tweens and scripted scrolls check `prefersReducedMotion()` themselves, because neither MotionConfig nor the CSS catch-all reaches them (TR-40). Scrolls use `smoothScrollBehavior()`: an explicit `behavior: 'smooth'` beats the catch-all's `scroll-behavior: auto`.
